DIRECTOR’S VISION

This is not a normal birthday website.

This should feel like a newly released mini Friends birthday episode where Ghazaal is introduced as the special new main character.

Imagine Netflix released a short interactive special:
Friends: The One Where Ghazaal Turns 29

The website should feel like:
- a mini episode
- an interactive apartment
- a birthday tribute
- a sitcom game
- a romantic message
- a teaser for next year’s bigger sequel

Episode 29 should feel warm, funny, personal, and magical.

Episode 30 should be teased as the blockbuster sequel: bigger, louder, golden, cinematic, dramatic, emotional, with more cast/friends/family involvement.


THE CORE EXPERIENCE

Ghazaal opens the link.

At first, it feels like a real episode is starting.

Then she enters the Friends apartment.

Then she realises the apartment is interactive.

Every object leads to a different “scene” from her birthday episode.

The site slowly reveals:
- why she is loved
- messages from friends
- memories
- AI scenes where she is in the Friends world
- jokes
- music
- little surprises
- Pouya’s final emotional message
- the bigger Episode 30 teaser

Final feeling:
She should laugh, smile, feel loved, and maybe cry a little.

FINAL DIRECTOR’S NOTE

This should feel like a small real episode, not a website.

Episode 29:
Warm, personal, funny, emotional, interactive.

Episode 30:
Clearly teased as the bigger sequel.

The strongest creative rule:
Every section should answer one question:
“Would this make Ghazaal feel like the main character of her own Friends birthday episode?”

If yes, keep it.
If no, cut it.
