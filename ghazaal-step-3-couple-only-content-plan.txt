POUYA FINAL MESSAGE DIRECTION

This is the main emotional moment.

Tone:
- sincere
- romantic
- warm
- slightly funny at the start
- not too formal
- not too long
- real

Length:
250 to 400 words.

Message structure:
1. “Ghazaal, this is The One Where You Turn 29.”
2. A normal birthday message was not enough.
3. Mention what makes her special.
4. Mention how she changes your everyday life.
5. Mention 29.
6. Say you are lucky.
7. Say this is only the first episode.
8. Tease the bigger 30.

Starter draft:
Ghazaal,

This is The One Where You Turn 29.

A normal birthday message felt too small, so I made you an episode. Very normal behaviour from me, obviously.

But honestly, I wanted to make something that feels even a little bit like you: warm, funny, beautiful, emotional, a bit dramatic in the best way, and impossible not to love.

You make ordinary days feel special. You make small moments feel like memories. You make rooms warmer just by being in them. And somehow, without even trying, you have become my favourite part of the story.

I love your laugh, your heart, your softness, your chaos, your kindness, and all the little things you probably do not even notice about yourself.

Happy 29th birthday, my love.

I hope this year gives you even a little bit of the happiness you give to the people around you.

And just so you know...

This is not the finale.

This is only Episode 1.

The big one is coming next year.
