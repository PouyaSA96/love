CORE IDEA

Create an interactive, cinematic, emotional, Friends-style birthday website where Ghazaal is the new main character in a small birthday episode.

The site should feel like:
- a new mini Friends episode
- an interactive apartment
- a birthday tribute
- a romantic surprise
- a sitcom-style experience
- the first chapter in a bigger birthday universe

Episode 29 is the warm first episode.

Episode 30 is the bigger sequel.


EPISODE 29 CREATIVE DIRECTION

Mood:
Warm, cute, sitcom-style, emotional, interactive, romantic, funny, personal, nostalgic, premium.

Visual world:
- Friends apartment
- Monica and Rachel apartment feeling
- purple door
- yellow frame
- couch
- coffee table
- warm apartment lighting
- birthday cake
- balloons
- framed photos
- kitchen/fridge details
- Central Perk coffee shop energy
- orange couch / cafe atmosphere
- episode title cards
- sitcom transitions
- laugh-track moments
- emotional end-of-episode moment

Story:
Ghazaal opens the site and slowly discovers she is the star of her own Friends birthday episode.

Everything in the apartment is personalised for her:

- the photo frame opens memories
- the TV opens the birthday trailer
- the couch opens Pouya’s message

EPISODE 30 CREATIVE DIRECTION

Bigger, more dramatic, more golden, more cinematic, funnier, louder, more emotional, more people, more videos, more surprises.

Visual world:
- Friends apartment, but bigger and more cinematic
- golden 30 teaser
- dramatic birthday sequel energy

- more AI videos
- bigger trailer
- stronger emotional finale

Story:
Episode 30 should feel like the sequel that was clearly planned from Episode 29.

Episode 29 should keep hinting:
