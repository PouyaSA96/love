29 REASONS COLLECTION

Section:
The One With 29 Reasons

Goal:
Create 29 reasons Pouya loves Ghazaal.

Tone:
- personal
- romantic
- funny
- not generic
- not copy-paste
- specific to her

Structure:
Reasons 1–8:
Cute and easy.

Reasons 9–18:
Funny, specific, inside-joke style.

Reasons 19–27:
Deeper and more emotional.

Reason 28:
Very romantic.

Reason 29:
Strongest final reason.

Template:
{
  "number": 1,
  "title": "Your laugh",
  "text": "The way your laugh makes the whole room feel warmer."
}

Good reason examples:
1. Your laugh.
2. The way you care about people.
3. Your smile.
4. Your little chaotic moments.
5. The way you make ordinary days feel special.
6. Your main-character energy.
7. The way you love your friends.
8. The way you make people feel comfortable.
9. Your soft side.
10. Your funny side.
11. The way you make me feel lucky.
12. The fact that life feels better with you in it.

Bad reason examples:
1. You are nice.
2. You are beautiful.
3. You are good.
4. You are cool.

Those are too generic. Use real memories and details.

Pouya task:
Write rough bullet points first. They do not need to be perfect.
Claude can polish later, but the real emotion must come from Pouya.
